Aki my Cutieful Cat

Created by github user @basilf1sh

This is my cat Aki! She is seven years old and one of the silliest cats I have ever had the pleasure to own. She is an American Shorthair with a grey tabby coat with a cute pink nose and the brightest green eyes!

I got her in 2019, two months short of graduating high school from my close friend Saul. A few months prior, his cat Avocado had a litter of kittens so I jumped at the chance to ask for one of them as our other cat Mooshie had been alone, so we decided to get her so that they could play together. Moo immediately took a liking to her, becoming best friends. Aki would always climb up my pant leg or lay on my chest while sleeping and then scream at me in the mornings to feed her, but I loved her so much.

Around the time Aki turned 10 months old though, she had a medical scare with her kidneys, as they were inflamed and it was affecting her greatly. I was afraid to lose her so soon, so I locked in and learned how to inject her with IV fluids to flush out her kidneys. After about a month she was back to her normal self and since then we have had no medical scares. Im very thankful she is able to live as long as she has with many more years to come.
